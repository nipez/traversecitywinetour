Wild Ales and Dining on Old Mission Peninsula

Jolly Pumpkin Restaurant & Brewery at 13512 Peninsula Drive is a full-service restaurant and artisan brewery on Old Mission Peninsula. It is known for wild-fermented, Belgian-inspired ales made with native yeasts and aged in oak barrels. The pour list also features beers from Jolly Pumpkin Artisan Ales, North Peak Brewing, and Old Mission Beer Co., which makes it a useful single stop when your group wants variety without leaving the peninsula.

Amenities: full kitchen, Old Mission Peninsula location, artisan beer, bar and dining, patio. Hours listed: Sun–Thu 11:30am–9pm · Fri–Sat 11:30am–10pm. Unlike many TC taprooms, this is explicitly a dining room destination — plan meal timing around tasting-room hours up the road.

Anchor Lunch Between OMP Wineries

Jolly Pumpkin sits among Old Mission estates such as Chateau Grand Traverse, Chateau Chantal, Brys Estate, Bowers Harbor, and Mari Vineyards. Use it as a sit-down lunch or early dinner between vineyard stops so you are not racing back downtown for food.

For chauffeured OMP days, see Old Mission tour guides, the hub at wine tour guides, and wine tours. Self-planners can mix winery pins in Plan Your Day and still leave a real meal slot here. Broader directories: wineries, breweries.

Planning Your Visit

Address: 13512 Peninsula Dr, Traverse City, MI 49686. Phone: (231) 223-4333. Confirm patio seating and kitchen hours seasonally. Menu and beer prices are set by the restaurant — ask them; we do not invent tasting fees.

Reservations may help for larger parties or peak summer evenings even when walk-ins are accepted for the bar — call to ask. Wild and oak-aged beers reward slower tasting; order flights or smaller pours if you still have winery stops ahead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jolly Pumpkin a brewery or a restaurant?

Both — a full-service restaurant and artisan brewery on Old Mission Peninsula with bar and dining spaces plus a patio.

What beer styles define Jolly Pumpkin?

Wild-fermented, Belgian-inspired, oak-aged ales are the signature, with additional pours from related Northern Michigan brands on offer.

Can I visit between winery stops?

Yes — it is a strong lunch or dinner anchor among OMP tasting rooms. See the Old Mission Peninsula winery list to build the rest of the day.

Do I need a reservation?

Policies vary by season and party size. Call (231) 223-4333 to ask about dining reservations versus bar walk-ins.

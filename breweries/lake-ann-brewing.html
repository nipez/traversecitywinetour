Small-Batch Charm Fifteen Minutes From TC

Lake Ann Brewing Company at 6535 First Street sits in the village of Lake Ann — about 15 minutes from Traverse City — with a relaxed taproom across from a park and a small rotating seasonal tap list. The adjacent Stone Oven restaurant means you can bring wood-fired food into the brewery, which fits the outside-food-welcome amenity and keeps the stop flexible for families and low-key groups.

Hours listed: Thu–Fri 3–9pm · Sat 12–9pm · Sun 12–6pm. That narrower weekly window matters: do not assume a Monday–Wednesday drop-in from our dataset. Amenities: taproom, park across the street, outside food welcome, dog friendly. TART Trail adjacency is part of the known-for list for visitors combining bikes or long walks with a pint.

Side Trip From the Wine Trail

Lake Ann is a deliberate detour from peninsula wine tasting — worth it when you want small-community atmosphere instead of another downtown brewpub. Afterward you can return to TC for Workshop, Ferment, or cider at city cideries. Peninsula days still center on Old Mission and Leelanau.

Plot drive time in Plan Your Day. If the rest of your itinerary is winery-dense, keep Lake Ann for a separate afternoon and use wine tours / tour guides on the tasting-heavy day.

Planning Your Visit

Address: 6535 First St, Lake Ann, MI 49650. Phone: (231) 640-2327. Confirm open days — the listed schedule is not seven days. Ask about outside food from Stone Oven and any pour pricing; we do not invent fees.

Walk-ins suit the small taproom. Dog-friendly and park-across-the-street amenities encourage a slower pace. Bring a designated driver for the return to Traverse City.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Lake Ann Brewing from Traverse City?

About 15 minutes to the village of Lake Ann. It is a short side trip, not a peninsula drive.

Can I bring food into the taproom?

Outside food welcome is listed, and the adjacent Stone Oven restaurant is the usual pairing — confirm current policy with staff.

What days is it open?

Our listings show Thu–Fri 3–9pm, Sat 12–9pm, Sun 12–6pm. Always verify before you drive out.

Is it dog friendly?

Dog friendly is listed. Check with the taproom for indoor versus patio rules.

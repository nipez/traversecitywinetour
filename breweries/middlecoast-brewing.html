MiddleCoast Brewing Company

State Street Market Taproom

MiddleCoast Brewing Company pours inside the State Street Market food hall at 329 E State Street — an industrial-chic downtown setting where approachable craft beer shares the building with multiple food vendors and outdoor seating. Beach Please Blonde Ale and All Day Vacay IPA are summer staples on the reputation board; expect a rotating list that favors easy-drinking styles over heavy experimental flights.

Amenities: taproom, food hall, outdoor seating, live music, downtown location. Hours listed: Mon–Thu 3–10pm · Fri 12–11pm · Sat 11am–11pm · Sun 11am–9pm. The food-hall model means you can eat from neighboring vendors without needing a separate restaurant reservation — useful when your wine day already filled the calendar.

Easy Add-On After Peninsula Tastings

MiddleCoast is a low-friction downtown stop after winery hopping or a short walk from Rare Bird and North Peak. Cider fans can add Taproot Cider House on Front Street without leaving the core. For peninsula routing help, see Old Mission and Leelanau pages, then stitch beer into Plan Your Day.

Prefer not to drive after tastings? Compare wine tour guides and book through wine tours for the vineyard legs, then walk to MiddleCoast once you are back downtown.

Planning Your Visit

Address: 329 E State St, Traverse City, MI 49684. Phone: (231) 421-8271. Confirm hours and live-music nights with the brewery. Pour and vendor food prices vary — ask on site; we do not invent fees.

Walk-ins suit the food-hall format. Peak summer weekends get busy; outdoor seating helps when the hall is full. Bring flexibility if you are meeting a larger group.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MiddleCoast inside a food hall?

Yes — State Street Market, with multiple food vendors and outdoor seating alongside the taproom.

What beers should I look for?

Beach Please Blonde Ale and All Day Vacay IPA are frequently cited summer pours. Check the board for what is actually on tap.

Does MiddleCoast have its own kitchen?

The setting is a food hall with vendors rather than a traditional single-kitchen brewpub. Eat from the market options on site.

How does it fit with wine tasting?

Use it as a downtown evening stop after peninsula wineries. Sequence stops in Plan Your Day or keep vineyard driving on a tour.

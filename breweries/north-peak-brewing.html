North Peak Brewing Company

← Back to All Breweries

A downtown TC staple housed in a century-old candy factory. North Peak celebrates Northern Michigan with a cherry porter, local walleye fish fry, cheddar ale soup, and a rotating lineup of craft beers. A full pub menu and prime Front Street location make it one of TC's most visited spots.

Front Street Pub in a Former Candy Factory

North Peak Brewing Company occupies a century-old candy factory at 400 W Front Street — one of downtown Traverse City's most visited brewpub addresses. The beer list leans into Northern Michigan themes, including a cherry porter, while the full pub menu (local walleye fish fry, cheddar ale soup, and more) makes it a practical lunch or dinner stop after tasting rooms close or before an evening walk along the bay.

Amenities include a full kitchen, deck seating, mug club, family-friendly dining, and a prime downtown location. Hours listed: Sun–Thu 11:30am–10pm · Fri–Sat 11:30am–11pm. That daytime open makes North Peak easier to schedule around winery hours than taprooms that start at 3pm.

Downtown Hub for Mixed Beverage Days

From North Peak you can walk to Rare Bird, MiddleCoast inside State Street Market, and Front Street cider houses like Taproot and Northern Natural. Wine-focused days usually put North Peak after Old Mission or Leelanau — or as a rainy-day downtown substitute when peninsula weather turns.

Planning Your Visit

Address: 400 W Front St, Traverse City, MI 49684. Phone: (231) 941-7325. Confirm hours and deck availability seasonally. Menu and beer prices come from the restaurant — ask them directly; we do not list invented tasting fees.

Walk-ins are common, but peak summer evenings and fish-fry nights can mean waits. Mug club and family dining skew this toward a restaurant visit more than a standing taproom flight.

Frequently Asked Questions

What beer is North Peak known for?

Cherry porter and other Northern Michigan–inspired beers headline the reputation, alongside a rotating craft lineup. Ask what is on draft the day you visit.

Is there a full menu?

Yes — full kitchen with pub staples such as walleye fish fry and cheddar ale soup. It works as a meal destination, not only a pour stop.

Does North Peak have outdoor seating?

Deck seating is listed among amenities. Availability depends on weather and season — ask when you arrive.

Can families visit?

Family friendly is listed. Confirm any age policies for the bar versus dining areas with staff.

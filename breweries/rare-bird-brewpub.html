Rare Bird Brewpub

← Back to All Breweries

Downtown Craft Beer With a Michigan Ingredient Focus

Rare Bird Brewpub sits on Lake Avenue in a restored 1931 red brick building, right in the walkable core of downtown Traverse City. The brewery leans hard into Michigan-grown hops and malt for its house beers, then supplements with a rotating craft selection across more than 35 taps. That combination makes it useful whether you want a house pint tied to local agriculture or a broader tasting flight before dinner on Front Street.

The kitchen is a real reason to linger. Rare Bird's food program is a standout among TC brewpubs — the Korean Pork Belly tacos are the dish people mention first — so this stop works as a meal destination rather than a quick pour-and-go. Amenities include a full kitchen, a dog-friendly patio, and live events, which fits an evening rhythm after a peninsula tasting day.

How Rare Bird Fits a Wine-and-Beer Trip

Many visitors build Traverse City days around Old Mission and Leelanau wineries, then return downtown for dinner and beer. Rare Bird slots cleanly into that pattern: close to lodging, walkable from other Front Street stops, and complementary rather than competitive with wine. Pair it with nearby North Peak Brewing on West Front Street or Workshop Brewing in the Warehouse District if you want a short downtown brewery loop.

If cider is also on your list, Taproot Cider House and Northern Natural Cider House are both downtown and easy to combine on foot. For a full peninsula-plus-city plan, use Plan Your Day or browse wine tour guide options when you would rather not drive between tasting rooms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Rare Bird Brewpub serve food?

Yes. The brewpub has a full kitchen, and the Korean Pork Belly tacos are a signature mention in local craft-beer conversations. Confirm current menu hours with the venue.

Does Rare Bird use Michigan ingredients?

House beers emphasize Michigan-grown hops and malt, alongside a rotating craft selection on 35+ taps. Ask the bar staff which pours are currently house-brewed.

Is the patio dog friendly?

Amenities list a dog-friendly patio. Policies can change — check with staff when you arrive, especially during busy weekend evenings.

How do I combine Rare Bird with a wine day?

Visit peninsula tasting rooms earlier, then finish downtown. Use Plan Your Day to sequence wineries, cideries, and brewpubs, or book a wine tour if you want a driver for the peninsula portion.

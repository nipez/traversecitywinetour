Brewery · Traverse City

Right Brain Brewery

← Back to All Breweries

One of TC's most creative breweries — Right Brain grows many of its own ingredients on-property including fruits, vegetables, flowers, honey, and hops. Located off Boardman Lake with a massive rotating tap list and an artistic, community-focused taproom known for wildly experimental recipes.

Known For

Barrel Aged Maple Cherry PotionKarma Palace IPAFarm-Grown Ingredients30+ Rotating Taps

What to Expect

Traverse City has been called one of America's great craft beer destinations — and Right Brain Brewery is part of that story. Whether you're a dedicated beer enthusiast or simply looking to explore TC's broader craft beverage scene alongside the wine trail, this is a stop worth making.

Planning Your Visit

Hours vary seasonally — confirm directly with the brewery before your visit. Walk-ins are welcome during regular taproom hours. Groups and events may require reservations; contact them directly for details.

Consider pairing your brewery visit with nearby wineries or cideries to make a full craft beverage day. Our interactive planner lets you mix and match stops across all venue types.

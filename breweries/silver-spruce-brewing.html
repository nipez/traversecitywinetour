Two TC Taprooms Focused on Classic Styles

Silver Spruce Brewing Company operates two Traverse City taprooms (8th Street and Silver Lake Road) with an emphasis on approachable, well-crafted classic styles — light lagers through robust stouts — rather than trend chasing. Two locations make it an easy add whenever your lodging or dinner plans already put you on that side of town.

Amenities: two taprooms, dog friendly, outdoor seating, community events. Hours listed as daily 12–9pm with variation by location — call (231) 421-8050 to confirm which room and that day's schedule. The “brew every style well” ethos pairs well with visitors who want a clean lager or stout after a day of sweeter fruit wines or cider.

Flexible Stops Around Town

Use Silver Spruce when downtown Front Street feels saturated. Combine with Earthen Ales (also multi-location) or Ferment for a non-downtown beer circuit. Wine and cider still anchor most visitor days via wineries, cideries, Old Mission, and Leelanau.

Build logistics in Plan Your Day. Keep vineyard driving on wine tours with help from tour guides when your group is tasting heavily before beer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Silver Spruce locations are in Traverse City?

Two taprooms — on 8th Street and Silver Lake Road. Hours can vary by location.

What beer styles should I expect?

Classic, approachable styles from lagers to stouts, with an emphasis on executing standards well rather than chasing trends.

Is Silver Spruce dog friendly?

Dog friendly and outdoor seating are listed. Confirm rules at the location you visit.

When should I choose Silver Spruce over downtown brewpubs?

When you want classic styles near your side of town or a quieter alternative to Front Street. Still pair wine days via Plan Your Day.

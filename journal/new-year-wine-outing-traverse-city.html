New Year Wine Outing in Traverse City: Plan the Toast Without Guessing Hours

~10 min read  ·  November 2026  ·  Traverse City Wine Country Journal
Home› Journal› New Year Wine Outing

Holiday-week and New Year wine outings are some of the most searched winter trips in Traverse City — and some of the easiest to overbook. Between office parties, visiting family, and New Year's Eve celebrations, tasting rooms may post special hours or closures that do not match a normal January Saturday.

This guide helps you plan a New Year (or late-holiday) wine outing without inventing hours. Pair it with our group wine tour guide if you are bringing a whole party, and the winter wineries guide for cold-season logistics.

Holiday hours are winery-specific. Example: Peninsula Cellars publicly lists Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day, and Easter among closures, with shortened Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve windows on their visit page. Other estates differ — verify yours.

What changes around New Year

  • Some tasting rooms close New Year's Day or run shortened Eve hours
  • Private vehicles and join-in seats for holiday-adjacent Saturdays book earlier
  • Downtown lodging fills with ski and festival visitors — book stays before tasting rooms
  • Weather can strand an ambitious Leelanau loop; keep a downtown backup dinner

Build the outing in this order

  1. Pick the date window (New Year's Eve afternoon, New Year's Day, or the weekend after)
  2. Book lodging near your peninsula — see where to stay
  3. Decide join-in vs private using Traverse City wine tours
  4. Shortlist two to three tasting rooms and confirm holiday hours on each winery site
  5. Sanity-check drive times in Plan Your Day

Route ideas that stay realistic

1
Old Mission half-day

Two confirmed stops + early dinner downtown

Start with a verified Old Mission room such as Chateau Grand Traverse or Bowers Harbor, add one short hop only if hours allow, then return before dark.

2
Sparkling-focused Leelanau

One estate anchor + optional second pour

L. Mawby is a natural festive stop when open; pair with Black Star Farms only after both confirm. Skip a third stop on holiday weeks.

Group tip

If the outing is an office party or reunion, read the group tour guide for headcount tiers and vehicle sizing before you deposit.

Book the sober ride first

Holiday weekends are when private SUVs and Sprinters disappear. Compare operators and request a quote while tasting-room hours are still being confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Are wineries open on New Year's Day in Traverse City?

It depends on the estate. Some publish New Year's Day as a closure; others post shortened holiday hours. Always check the winery's site for the specific date — do not assume a normal winter Saturday schedule.

When should we book a New Year wine outing?

Lock lodging and a driver first for holiday-adjacent weekends, then confirm tasting rooms. Popular Saturday vehicles fill earlier than midweek afternoons.

Old Mission or Leelanau for a holiday-week wine day?

Old Mission is usually the safer short-day choice near downtown. Save Leelanau for a clearer forecast and confirmed hours.

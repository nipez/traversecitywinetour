Plan a Spring Wine Trip to Traverse City Before Peak Season

~11 min read  ·  November 2026  ·  Traverse City Wine Country Journal
Home› Journal› Spring Wine Trip Planning

If you are aiming six to eight weeks ahead from late fall, you are really planning late winter into early spring — the moment Traverse City wine country starts waking up but is not yet peak-season crowded. This guide is for booking a spring wine trip (roughly April–May) without treating summer itineraries as a template.

Use it alongside the first-timer tasting guide and Old Mission vs Leelanau. For current winter conditions on the ground before your trip, keep the winter wineries guide handy.

Spring hours are not universal. Example pattern from published schedules: Chateau Grand Traverse lists a distinct spring window before summer hours; Peninsula Cellars publishes a November–May daily window that differs from its June–October hours. Always read the estate you plan to visit.

Why book the spring trip now

  • Private SUVs and Sprinters for May Saturdays start disappearing while rates are still shoulder-season
  • Lodging near downtown and on-peninsula inns fills around festival and holiday weekends
  • You can shortlist tasting rooms now and reconfirm hours closer to travel — better than building the route the week of arrival

April vs May on the peninsulas

April

Quieter tasting rooms, more variable weather, and a higher chance some seasonal operations are still on winter footing. Ideal for unhurried pours if you confirm hours.

May

Longer days and more weekend energy before summer density. Still confirm openings — “spring” on a brochure is not the same as a live hours page.

How to build the booking stack

  1. Choose peninsula and trip length (one day vs weekend)
  2. Reserve lodging — vineyard inns and downtown hotels on where to stay
  3. Compare tour styles and request a quote on /wine-tours, or pick a bookable day under /tours/
  4. Shortlist four tasting rooms; confirm two to three as travel nears via each winery site and Open this week
  5. Map the drive with Plan Your Day

Frequently asked questions

When do Traverse City wineries expand hours in spring?

It varies by estate. Some publish distinct spring windows before full summer hours; others stay on a winter or November–May schedule until early summer. Check each winery — and watch our Open this week filter as dates approach.

Is April or May better for a wine trip?

April is quieter with more variable weather and hours. May usually brings longer days and more weekend energy while still beating July crowds. Confirm openings either way.

Should I book a wine tour before spring travel?

Yes for Friday–Saturday private vehicles and popular join-in seats, especially holiday weekends. Shoulder-season midweek dates are often more flexible.

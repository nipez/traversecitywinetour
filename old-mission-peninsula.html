Old Mission Peninsula Wineries

Home› Wineries› Old Mission Peninsula Peninsula

Stretching 18 miles into Grand Traverse Bay along the 45th parallel, Old Mission Peninsula is home to some of Michigan's finest wineries. The narrow peninsula's unique microclimate — moderated by water on both sides — produces exceptional Riesling, Pinot Noir, and Chardonnay.

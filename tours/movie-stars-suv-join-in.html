Movie Stars Wine Tours positions itself as the non-bus alternative: semi-private SUVs where couples may be combined, with private options up to 7 in one vehicle. Their site says wine tours are all they do, covering Old Mission and Leelanau plus distillery and brew options.

Day tours are typically four wineries over 4–5 hours; sunset-style products run shorter stop lists. They make winery reservations and pick up / drop off in downtown Traverse City.

This product is also listed on Viator and GetYourGuide, so you can book OTA checkout or go direct — or request a quote if you want a private vehicle instead of a combined join-in.

What’s included

  • Luxury SUV transportation (GMC Yukon / Chevrolet Suburban; Escalade products listed on their catalog)
  • Local wine guides
  • Wine samples / “splashes” as described on their site
  • Complimentary bottle for groups of 6+ (FAQ)

What’s not included (tasting fees)

  • Flights, bottles, and food (paid at wineries)
  • Recommended ~20% guide gratuity

Sources: moviestarswinetours.com, Book Now catalog, and FAQ.

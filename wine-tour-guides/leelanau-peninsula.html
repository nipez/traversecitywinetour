Leelanau Peninsula wine tour operators earn their keep on distance. Tasting rooms stretch from the base of the peninsula near Suttons Bay toward Lake Leelanau, Northport, and the Lake Michigan shore towns. A guided day here feels more like a road-trip tasting loop than a downtown afternoon — which is exactly why visitors search to compare Leelanau wine tour companies before they book.

Compare against Old Mission Peninsula guides, return to the tour guides hub, or read the peninsula overview on Leelanau Peninsula. For sparkling-focused planning, see our journal piece on L. Mawby Michigan sparkling wine.

Build buffer into the map

Rural roads, photo pulls, and tasting rooms that run long all add minutes. Ask your guide to plan fewer stops well rather than six rushed pours.

Town + estate rhythm

Many strong Leelanau days alternate an estate pour with a short village stretch — Suttons Bay or Lake Leelanau — without turning the tour into a shopping crawl.

  2. Fieldstone whites & Pinot: Shady Lane Cellars
  3. Sparkling stop: L. Mawby
  4. Lake views: Bel Lago or ridge pour at Rove Estate
  5. Optional closer: Willow Vineyard or Forty-Five North if daylight and hours allow

FAQ

Leelanau tour guide FAQs

How long should a Leelanau wine tour be?

Leelanau is more spread out than Old Mission, so many groups prefer a longer window — often five or six hours — so drive time between estates does not eat the tasting schedule.

Which Leelanau stops do guided tours often include?

Common anchors include Black Star Farms, Shady Lane Cellars, L. Mawby for sparkling, Bel Lago, and Rove Estate. Exact stops depend on hours, reservations, and your taste profile.

Can a guide mix cider with wine on Leelanau?

Yes — several Leelanau destinations pour cider alongside wine. Tell your operator if you want a mixed beverage day so they can sequence stops that fit.

Need a bigger vehicle?

Bridal parties and large friend groups often need Sprinters, shuttles, or buses — see limo & bus tour guides.

Private & Group Wine Tours

When to book a private vehicle versus a join-in seat, with operators who offer each.

Home›Wine Tour Guides›Private & Group Tours
HubOld MissionLeelanauPrivate & GroupLimo & BusHow to Choose

Private and group wine tours in Traverse City solve different problems. Private means your guest list only — useful when you want a custom Old Mission or Leelanau sequence, a specific celebration vibe, or simply no strangers on the bus. Group or join-in means you share a scheduled vehicle with other parties, which can be the smarter buy for two people who do not need a full SUV.

This spoke sits under our wine tour guides hub. For peninsula routing, see Old Mission and Leelanau. Planning a bridal weekend? Pair this page with the Michigan winery bachelorette party guide and the bachelorette tour planner.

Choose private when…

You have 4+ friends with a shared taste profile, need custom timing around dinner reservations, or want a chauffeur who will hold winery bookings for your exact list.

Choose join-in when…

You are a couple or solo traveler, want a set price for a seat, and are fine with an operator-selected winery mix on Old Mission or Leelanau.

FAQ

Private vs group FAQs

What is the difference between a private and a join-in wine tour?

A private tour reserves the vehicle for your party only and usually lets you influence the winery list and pacing. A join-in (public) tour seats multiple parties together on an operator-chosen route — often cheaper per person for couples or solo travelers.

What group size fits a private Traverse City wine tour?

It depends on the vehicle. Published examples include SUVs for roughly 2–7 guests and Sprinters or shuttles that seat around 10–14. Larger parties may need multiple vehicles — ask the operator.

Are private tours only for bachelorette parties?

No. Operators market private tours for couples, families, corporate groups, and celebrations alike. Bachelorette groups are common, but not the only use case.

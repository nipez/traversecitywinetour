Choose a peninsula

Old Mission vs Leelanau

Old Mission Peninsula

Closer to downtown lodging, shorter hops between tasting rooms, and the easier half-day default. Strong for first visits, winter weather windows, and join-in buses that leave from the Civic Center. Start with the Old Mission 4-winery bus or a private SUV.

Leelanau Peninsula

More space between estates, lake and village stops, and a fuller day-trip feel. Better when you have daylight and want room to linger. Compare the Leelanau bus tour or build a private day via Leelanau tour guides.

Still choosing? Read Old Mission vs Leelanau or browse peninsula-focused operator lists in the guides hub.

FAQ

Traverse City wine tour questions

How much do Traverse City wine tours cost?

Published ranges vary by style. Join-in seats often start around $79–$89 per person (Magic Shuttle Bus from $79; Movie Stars SUV join-in from $89; TC Brew Bus join-in $82 + tax). Private SUVs start near $375 for three hours (Grand Traverse Rides) or $420 for a four-hour Wagoneer package (Veer Charters). Private Sprinters and vans commonly run $499–$799 for four hours depending on operator and day. Tasting fees are usually extra — confirm live rates before you deposit.

Should I book Old Mission or Leelanau for a wine tour?

Old Mission is closer to downtown Traverse City and suits shorter half-day tours. Leelanau is more spread out and rewards a longer day with village and lake stops. Many operators run both; choose based on time, scenery, and which tasting rooms you want.

What is the difference between a join-in tour and a private wine tour?

A join-in tour sells individual seats on a shared vehicle with a set itinerary and published per-person price. A private tour books the whole vehicle for your group, so you control pace, stops, and often peninsula choice — usually priced as a vehicle rate rather than per seat.
